# Stadium Announcer v1.0.3

Stadium Announcer brings Pokémon Stadium-style spoken battle commentary to Pokémon Red through Gen1Recomp.

## Highlights

- Complete 823-clip announcer voice pack included.
- Pokémon send-out calls for all 151 Generation I species.
- Move-name commentary for the Generation I move set.
- Reactions for critical hits, type effectiveness, status conditions, fainting, victories, and battle flow.
- Gym Leader, Elite Four, and Champion encounter introductions.
- Optional decision-screen idle commentary.
- Configurable announcer scope: major battles, all trainer battles, or all battles.
- Enemy send-out announcer timing fixed in v1.0.3.

## Installation

Download `StadiumAnnouncer-v1.0.3.zip` and install it as a Gen1Recomp mod. Enable **Stadium Announcer** in the mod list, then configure its battle scope in the mod options if desired.

## Compatibility

Stadium Announcer is audio-only and does not modify battle rendering, Pokémon sprites, move animations, arenas, the battle HUD, gameplay mechanics, or save data. It declares a conflict with `STADIUM_BATTLE_FX` because that project has overlapping announcer functionality.
